Selected work

Catching material search declines

Problem

Editors can check only a few pages per client. A visibility drop is worth investigating when the relative change and the absolute loss are both meaningful.

What I worked on

For my FlyRank ML capstone, I used Codex as a build partner to implement a time-separated evaluation and publish the reproducible analysis. The study compares a logistic ranker and a fixed exposure rule on the same June pages.

What came of it

The ranker found 218 material drops in 320 review slots. The rule found 103. In the pooled top 50, the rule’s hits covered more lost impressions. The full paper shows both measures.
